# VR playtest 01: first run in the headset

Build: any build from 2026-11-14 on. Time: about 30 minutes. Nothing here has been tried in a headset before, so
stop at the first step that is badly broken and send what you have.

**Start:** `CDDA-3D-x86_64.AppImage --vr` (Linux) or, from the repo, `Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --xr-mode on
--path client -- --live --vr`. **VR menu:** the left controller's menu button. **Quit:** close the game window on the
desktop (it saves), or take the headset off and close it there. **Report a bug:** the last row of the VR menu.

1. Start the game. Expect: the town in the headset within a few minutes, no endless "game booting". Happened: ____
2. Look around and walk a few steps for real. Expect: the world stays still and at a natural size. Happened: ____
3. Move with the stick and turn (snap turn by default). Expect: smooth movement, a soft vignette while moving, no
   lurch. Happened: ____
4. Open the VR menu, change "Snap angle", close it. Expect: turning uses the new angle. Happened: ____
5. Raise the off-hand wrist and turn its inside to your eyes. Expect: the wrist HUD (body, needs, messages) is
   readable without squinting. Happened: ____
6. Grip an item on a table, carry it, hand it to the other hand, let it go. Expect: it follows each hand and drops
   where you let go. Happened: ____
7. Reach over your shoulder and grip. Expect: the backpack opens in front of you; you can take an item out.
   Happened: ____
8. Do something that asks a question (eat, reload). Expect: a panel in front of you; answer it with the laser and
   trigger. Happened: ____
9. In the VR menu, try "Menu text size" from 80 to 160 %. Expect: the panel text changes size. Which reads best?
   Happened: ____
10. Wield a gun and fire at something. Expect: it fires where it points. Happened: ____
11. Swing a fist, then a held weapon, at a zombie next to you. Expect: one strike per swing, none while your hands
    rest near it. Happened: ____
12. Press "Report a bug" once, even if nothing went wrong. Expect: the hand says the report was saved. Happened: ____

**Comfort check:** any nausea, eye strain or arm tiredness? At which step? ____

**Send:** this file filled in, `vr_perf.log`, the `bug_reports/` folder, and the console output if you have it
(see README.md here for where they are).
